Learning is only the first half

TechBridge is a program of Baselink Services Limited that connects what people learn to the work they will do. Our practical internship gives learners a place to apply their knowledge to real projects.

It is made for learners and aspiring interns who want hands-on, project-based experience in technology.

What we offer

A 30-Day Practical Internship in Data Analytics or Web Development, built around real project work.

Who it is for

Learners and aspiring interns who want to move from knowing the theory to doing the work.

Why join

Build practical skills on real tasks, finish projects you can show, and you may earn a recommendation letter.
